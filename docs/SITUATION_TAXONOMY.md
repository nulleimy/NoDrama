# NoDrama — Situation Taxonomy

## Scope

This taxonomy covers public-safe NoDrama Reply categories only.

Parked for a separate future product/module:

- partnership
- high-sensitivity family conflict
- coercion/manipulation scenarios
- crisis scenarios
- medical/legal/police fake claims
- document forgery
- fraud

## Domains

- social
- work
- business
- money
- school
- digital

## Required category count

The first production target is at least 100 public-safe categories.

## Style system

Supported styles:

- neutral
- casual
- formal
- firm
- funny
- absurd

Funny and absurd styles are allowed mainly in safe social/digital contexts.

They are disabled by default for:

- work
- business
- email
- sensitive money contexts
