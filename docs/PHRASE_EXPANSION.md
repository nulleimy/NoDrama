# NoDrama — Phrase Expansion Engine

## Strategy

Bundle 6 adds a semi-automatic phrase expansion layer.

It does not use live AI. It combines curated phrase patterns into many candidate replies.

## Why

Manual quality is good but slow. Live AI is flexible but expensive and unpredictable.

The expansion engine gives NoDrama:

- more variety
- controlled humor
- deterministic output
- zero AI cost
- repeatable quality checks

## Expansion model

Each pattern contains:

- openings
- cores
- closings
- style
- intent
- channel
- language
- intensity
- risk

The engine combines them into phrase candidates and filters them through anti-cringe checks.

## Example

opening + core + closing

becomes one candidate reply.
