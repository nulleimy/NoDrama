# NoDrama — Language Sourcing Strategy

## Principle

NoDrama must not be a generic AI wrapper. It needs a curated language layer.

## Allowed sourcing

Use public language resources for inspiration, patterns, and linguistic reference:

- Czech National Corpus style research
- Internetova jazykova prirucka UJC for correctness
- Wiktionary / Wikislovnik for lexical reference
- Tatoeba for sentence-level inspiration
- OpenSubtitles-style corpora for conversational tone, after filtering
- public business email patterns as structural inspiration
- internal curated phrase writing

## Not allowed

Do not copy-paste:

- private messages
- copyrighted template packs
- Reddit comments verbatim
- meme pages verbatim
- personal user data
- sensitive relationship conversations

## Humor strategy

Funny and absurd replies should be based on reusable patterns, not copied jokes.

Preferred humor patterns:

- social battery
- low power mode
- internal management
- operating system maintenance
- temporary incompatibility
- scheduled or off-schedule human downtime

Avoid:

- death jokes
- illness jokes
- fake emergencies
- humiliation
- aggressive sarcasm
- long explanations of the joke

## Target language mix

The first target is 100 categories times 6 styles times 5 phrases, or roughly 3000 phrase candidates.

Live app usage can later identify top copied replies and promote them into the curated bank.
